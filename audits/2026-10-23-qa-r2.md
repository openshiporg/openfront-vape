## Major

### QA-026: Every dashboard form except the product page still hides validation errors behind "Unable to complete the request"
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URL:** for example `/dashboard/regulated-product-classifications/create`, `/dashboard/regulated-merchant-profiles/<id>`, `/dashboard/provider-approvals/create`, `/dashboard/jurisdictions/create`
- **Steps:** Fill in a compliance record the hooks will reject (for example a classification with an evidence digest that isn't 64 hex characters, or activating a merchant profile with no PACT evidence) and save.
- **Expected:** The hook's own message, which is written for merchants. That message is the only way to learn what the regulated rules want.
- **Actual (from code; I didn't submit live because of the no-write limit above):** `features/dashboard/lib/keystoneClient.ts:62-64` and `:135-137` still return `PUBLIC_REQUEST_ERROR` for every GraphQL error. Round 1 worked around it only for product saves (`features/platform/products/actions/compliance.ts`, `explainProductSaveError`). The Compliance Center now sends merchants to exactly these generic forms (Profiles, Rule sets, Classifications, Add approval). So the one place every fix leads still answers "Unable to complete the request".
- **Likely file:** `features/dashboard/lib/keystoneClient.ts`. Pass through errors whose `extensions.code` is `KS_VALIDATION_FAILURE` or `KS_ACCESS_DENIED` (already listed in `features/dashboard/lib/security.ts`). Then the product-only workaround can go.

### QA-027: Classifying a product, the step every product needs before it can be sold, is a raw developer form
- **Area:** dashboard (main merchant workflow)
- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/products/cmvlcvaf30001pr0x0fb60auf`, then Compliance, then **Classify** (goes to `/dashboard/regulated-product-classifications/create`)
- **Steps:** From the QA product's Compliance section, click Classify.
- **Expected:** A form already tied to that variant, with merchant labels ("Nicotine strength (mg/ml)", "Device type: Closed pod / Disposable / E-liquid …"), which then returns to the product.
- **Actual:**
  - The link is a bare `/create` with no variant, so the merchant has to search for the variant again in "Variant: Search…". After saving they don't come back to the product.
  - Enum values show raw: the Category default reads **"ends device"**, Nicotine Source "unknown", Marketing Review Status "pending", Status "draft".
  - Labels are schema names: "Nicotine Concentration Mg Ml", "Liquid Volume Ml", "Set field as null" under every select.
  - It asks for "Evidence Digest" and "Marketing Evidence Digest" (a 64-character SHA-256, per `explainProductSaveError`'s regex), with no hint of what that is or how to produce it.
  - The same applies to Product Authorizations, the other record the "No sellable product evidence" blocker needs.
- **Likely file:** `features/platform/products/components/ComplianceSection.tsx:58` (pass the variant id, for example `?productVariant=<id>`, and return the user afterwards). Field labels and enum display names go in the RegulatedProductClassification list config under `features/keystone/models/` (`ui.label` and the select option labels). A purpose-built classify sheet on the product page would match how E-commerce handles variants.

### QA-028: The shipping state is optional free text, but the compliance engine needs an exact state code
- **Area:** storefront (account addresses and checkout), compliance
- **URL:** `/us/account/addresses`, then Add address. Also `/us/checkout` (shipping address).
- **Steps:** Add an address and leave State empty, or type "Texas".
- **Expected:** For a US vape retailer the state decides whether the sale is legal (flavour bans, licensing, PACT/state shipping bans). It should be a required dropdown of state codes for US addresses.
- **Actual:** In both forms "State / Province" is labelled *optional* and is a plain text input. The enforcement builds the jurisdiction as `${country}-${province.toUpperCase()}` (`features/keystone/regulated/enforcement.ts:89-93`, `evidence-ingestion.ts:78-79`). So an empty state gives `null`, and "Texas" gives `US-TEXAS`, which can never match the jurisdiction `US-TX`. Either way the cart fails closed at checkout with no hint that the state field is the cause.
- **Likely file:** `features/storefront/modules/account/components/address-card/address-fields.tsx:99-106` (no `required`), `features/storefront/modules/checkout/components/shipping-address/index.tsx:166-171`. Use a required state select (ISO 3166-2 codes) when the country is US.

### QA-029: Customers must agree to a Privacy Policy and Terms that say "Not published yet", and nothing tells the merchant
- **Area:** storefront and dashboard
- **URL:** `/us/account`, then Create account, then "Privacy Policy" and "Terms of Use" links. Also `/us/policies/age-verification`, `/shipping` and `/returns`.
- **Steps:** Open any policy link on the live store.
- **Expected:** Either real policy text, or (while it's missing) the store can't take sign-ups or sales and the merchant is told to publish policies.
- **Actual:** All six policy pages render the heading and "Not published yet." The sign-up form still says "By creating an account you agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use". The footer hides the Policies column entirely while they're empty, so the store shows no shipping, returns or age-verification policy anywhere. The Compliance Center lists 8 blockers but none for missing policies, legal name or licences, and Store Settings has no "required" marker on them.
- **Likely file:** `features/storefront/screens/PolicyPage.tsx` and `features/storefront/modules/account/components/register/index.tsx:84-90` (hide the agreement sentence or block sign-up when unpublished). Add a "Policies and business details" readiness row to `features/platform/compliance-center/screens/ComplianceCenterPage.tsx`, or a warning on Store Settings.
