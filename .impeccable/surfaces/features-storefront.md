# Surface: vape storefront (`features/storefront/**`, `app/(storefront)/**`, the `vp-*` section of `app/globals.css`)

Mode: Persuade on home; Operate on store, category, product, cart, checkout, account. Adult shopper (21+) finding and reordering devices, e-liquids and accessories, mostly on a phone. Inferred without interview (owner ordered no approval stage). Replaces the 2026-10-25 "spec sheet at night" world, which the owner rejected.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The shop's front window. A vape shop announces itself through its glass: the name in gold-leaf sign-painter lettering, an OPEN neon, the 21+ notice in window vinyl, and the shelves visible behind; the storefront opens as that window (dark glass band with gold lettering and the one neon) and then steps inside to bright shelves, instead of the category default of an all-dark page with an oversized headline over a category list.
OWN-WORLD: Window bands in deep glass green-black; gold leaf (flat gold, never gradient) for display lettering on glass; the logo hue as the single neon line, set beneath the gold name (the tagline, or the open state when the data proves it), one per screen; everything inside is bright: cool white shelves, ink text, hairline rules. Big Shoulders Display (Chicago sign-painting lineage) for display and headings, Hanken Grotesk body, Spline Sans Mono for strength (mg/mL), sizes, prices. 4px plates and panels, joined square segmented controls, no pills.
STORY: The shopper sees which shop, that it is 21+, and the departments painted on the glass; steps in to the shelves by category, scans products by name, strength and price, adds to cart, checks out.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass band: store name in gold lettering, the neon line beneath it, the departments painted as a window list each a link, the 21+ vinyl note; below the band, the first shelf of products on white with category headings; phone: band shortens to name + departments list, shelf follows.
